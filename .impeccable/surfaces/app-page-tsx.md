Scope: the whole portfolio (home, profile, projects index, project detail, contact). Visitor mode: Experience.

Audience and job: recruiters and engineering leads judging depth fast, then contacting Abrar or opening the résumé. Proof: real employers, real figures, real screenshots. Constraint: complete without WebGL or motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A career is a transit network you can read. Each stack is a line, each project or employer a station, and interchanges show where stacks meet. The map is generated from the data, so it is an accurate index and not decoration. Refuses the dark hero, stat row and card grid of the category, and the old game language.

OWN-WORLD: Vitreous enamel ground, black enamel signs, seven saturated line colours as whole-page fields, white station capsules, round lettered line bullets. Archivo at wide widths as signage voice, Geist Mono only for dates and data. Screenshots hang in black poster frames. Year bands sit on the map as fare zones. Night theme is the same system underground. Full colour marks the active line and dims the rest.

STORY: The visitor sees the whole career as one object, picks a line or station, flies to it, reads the evidence, and every route ends at the hollow "Next stop" station and the contact form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed real-time 3D enamel model fills the screen above a full-width black station sign at the bottom holding the name at display scale, the line bullets as working filters, one sentence of positioning, and the two actions: see the work, résumé. Station labels are real links on the model, with Psycloud and the Prime Minister's Office carrying their figures. On load the flat diagram rises into the model.

FORM: Modernist subway diagram as enamel model; position 4 on my ordered list; seed:48f632bd27ee8846. Signature interaction: choosing a line bullet raises that line and drains the others, then a focused station lifts and the camera flies to it. Raises: one unbroken route line with a train down every page; numbered and lettered keyboard exits; a swatch lights every region it governs; origin ticket on each station; one colour reserved for what is active, asymmetry as a rule.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
